## 5. What-If Scenario Sensitivity Findings
- **Discount Discipline (-10% Discounts)**: Reduces commercial discounting by \$920.5k, directly increasing operating profit from \$16.89M to **\$17.81M** (a $+5.45\%$ profit surge) and expanding profit margin to $14.89\%$.
- **COGS Inflation (+5% Cost Inflation)**: Increases COGS by \$5.09M, crushing corporate profit down to **\$11.80M** (a $-30.1\%$ profit destruction) and contracting margin to $9.94\%$.
- **Actionable Strategic Imperative**: Because COGS absorbs $85.77\%$ of revenue, corporate profitability is $5.5\times$ more sensitive to manufacturing cost inflation than to equivalent percentage shifts in discount policy.
